# PitchSense AI — Competitive Intelligence Master Report (50 Companies)

**Research dates:** Batch 1 October 17, 2026; Batches 2–3 October 18, 2026; Batch 4 October 19, 2026 (Europe/Berlin).
**Scope:** 50 companies competing directly or indirectly with PitchSense AI, researched in four batches and consolidated here. PitchSense's four intelligence areas — Call Intelligence, Deal Intelligence, Content Intelligence, Coaching Intelligence — connected by an AI agent through shared organizational memory.
**Framing:** This is source-cited desk research based on official company pages and documentation, with explicitly unresolved capabilities flagged as unverified — not an exhaustively verified product audit.
**Product-vision caveat:** PitchSense's description includes built, in-development, and planned capabilities; the full description is treated as the product vision, not proof that every capability is currently available. Scores below describe competitors only.

## Scoring rubric (frozen across all batches)

- **Call Intelligence (CI):** 0 = none documented; 1 = partial (e.g., recording or transcription or analysis, but not a full capture-to-insight loop); 2 = full conversation intelligence (recording/capture, analysis, and reuse in workflows).
- **Deal Intelligence (DI):** 0 = none; 1 = partial (notes export or generic pipeline views alone do not qualify; requires opportunity fields, history, stakeholders, risks, or actions); 2 = full (deal-level insights, risk/forecast support, or structured CRM writes).
- **Content Intelligence (CoI):** 0 = none; 1 = partial (discovery, approved-document grounding, recommendations, or governed materials in limited scope); 2 = full (content library/grounding connected to sales workflows with recommendations or generation).
- **Coaching Intelligence (ChI):** 0 = none; 1 = partial (coaching recommendations or scorecards without practice); 2 = full (roleplay/practice plus feedback/certification, or verified coaching workflows). Roleplay alone is Coaching, not Call Intelligence.
- **Shared Memory (SM):** 0 = none documented; 1 = retained context in limited scope reused in workflows (the word "memory" is not required; a chatbot, CRM sync, or AI search alone is insufficient); 2 = persistent cross-source context used across capabilities.
- Maximum 10. Only documented product evidence is used. 1 means documented partial capability, not uncertainty; uncertain depth or availability is flagged separately.

Ranked by documented capability overlap with PitchSense's four intelligences plus shared memory, weighted for directness and ICP fit:

1. **Gong (8)** — 2/2/0/2/2. The only vendor scoring 2 on four of five dimensions; the Revenue Graph is a de facto organizational memory layer ([Revenue Graph](https://www.gong.io/platform/revenue-graph)).

9. **Traq.ai (6)** — 2/2/0/1/1. Full CI plus deal intelligence from calls with a sales-org ICP ([product](https://www.traq.ai/product/)).
10. **Avoma (6)** — 2/2/0/1/1. Meeting assistant plus revenue intelligence with a deal copilot ([revenue intelligence](https://www.avoma.com/revenue-intelligence)).

Challengers: Backstory (6; CRO-first answers on activity memory, [backstory.ai](https://www.backstory.ai/)), Jiminny (6, [revenue intelligence](https://jiminny.com/product/revenue-intelligence)), Zoom Revenue Accelerator (6; strongest suite entrant — CI 2 + DI 2 inside Zoom, [product](https://www.zoom.com/en/products/conversation-intelligence/)), Salesloft + Clari (5; direct but content/coaching gaps, [merger page](https://www.salesloft.com/clari-salesloft-merger)). ### Three potential market gaps (hypotheses to test — not verified absences)

1. **Conversation memory connected to approved content.** Enablement suites (Showpad, Mindtickle, Allego, Seismic) hold approved content; CI platforms (Gong, Traq, Jiminny) hold conversation memory. The reviewed sources did not establish a closed loop in which call-derived memory drives content recommendations (and content usage informs coaching) for live deals. Validate against the nearest partial counterexamples: Attention's Collateral Genie (deck generation from deal context, [agents](https://www.attention.com/product/agents-directory)), Showpad's Genie (grounded in "proprietary knowledge", [Genie](https://www.showpad.com/showpad-genie)), and Allego's conversation-to-content flows ([allego.com](https://www.allego.com/)).
2. **A sales-org memory layer spanning calls, emails, CRM, and content.** Strongest claims are conversation-scoped (Fireflies, Noota, Circleback, tl;dv) or suite/platform-scoped (Agentforce, Zoom). The reviewed sources did not establish persistent, org-wide context reused across all four intelligence areas for a sales organization. Validate against the nearest partial counterexamples: Sybill's "shared memory" ([sybill.ai](https://www.sybill.ai/)), Aida's Digital Twin ([platform](https://getaida.com/platform)), Gong's Revenue Graph ([Revenue Graph](https://www.gong.io/platform/revenue-graph)), and Aviso's cross-dataset platform ([platform](https://www.aviso.com/platform)) — each covers a subset of sources or workflows.
3. **Deal-grounded content generation with governance.** The reviewed sources did not establish customer-facing document generation grounded in both deal history and approved content libraries with governance. Validate against the nearest partial counterexamples: Attention's deal-context decks ([agents](https://www.attention.com/product/agents-directory)) and Accent's on-brand generation from its knowledgebase ([platform](https://accent-technologies.com/platform/)) — each covers half of the requirement.

Per the research brief, no PitchSense positioning recommendation is made here; these gaps are inputs to that decision, not conclusions about unoccupied market space.
