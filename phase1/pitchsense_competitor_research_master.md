## Scoring rubric (frozen across all batches)

- **Call Intelligence (CI):** 0 = none documented; 1 = partial (e.g., recording or transcription or analysis, but not a full capture-to-insight loop); 2 = full conversation intelligence (recording/capture, analysis, and reuse in workflows).
- **Deal Intelligence (DI):** 0 = none; 1 = partial (notes export or generic pipeline views alone do not qualify; requires opportunity fields, history, stakeholders, risks, or actions); 2 = full (deal-level insights, risk/forecast support, or structured CRM writes).
- **Content Intelligence (CoI):** 0 = none; 1 = partial (discovery, approved-document grounding, recommendations, or governed materials in limited scope); 2 = full (content library/grounding connected to sales workflows with recommendations or generation).
- **Coaching Intelligence (ChI):** 0 = none; 1 = partial (coaching recommendations or scorecards without practice); 2 = full (roleplay/practice plus feedback/certification, or verified coaching workflows). Roleplay alone is Coaching, not Call Intelligence.
- **Shared Memory (SM):** 0 = none documented; 1 = retained context in limited scope reused in workflows (the word "memory" is not required; a chatbot, CRM sync, or AI search alone is insufficient); 2 = persistent cross-source context used across capabilities.
- Maximum 10. Only documented product evidence is used. 1 means documented partial capability, not uncertainty; uncertain depth or availability is flagged separately.

### Three potential market gaps (hypotheses to test — not verified absences)

1. **Conversation memory connected to approved content.** Enablement suites (Showpad, Mindtickle, Allego, Seismic) hold approved content; CI platforms (Gong, Traq, Jiminny) hold conversation memory.
