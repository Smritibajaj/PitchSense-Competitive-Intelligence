## Batch 2 of 4: Conversation Intelligence — 9 companies (cumulative: 23 of 50)

**Research date:** October 18, 2026 (batch 1: October 17, 2026)

**Scope and framing:** Same standards as batch 1 — official pages only, vendor claims not independently tested, 0 = "not verified in the reviewed sources" (not proven absence), the PitchSense narrative treated as vision. Shared Memory is scored functionally (1 = retained context in limited scope reused in workflows; 2 = persistent cross-source context used across capabilities). Category, directness, and rankings are analyst assessments. Statements labeled "analyst note" are observations about page content, not vendor quotations.

### 15. ZoomInfo (Chorus offering)
- **Category:** Conversation Intelligence (company: ZoomInfo; product: Chorus)
- **ICP:** B2B "performance-driven sales teams"; no company-size criteria stated ([product page](https://www.zoominfo.com/products/chorus)).
- **Documented audiences:** sales teams, go-to-market teams; testimonials from Revenue Enablement Supervisor, VP Sales & Success, Associate Training Manager, Account Executive ([product page](https://www.zoominfo.com/products/chorus)). Likely economic buyer (analyst inference): VP Sales / RevOps.
- **Daily users:** salespeople, GTM team members.
- **Pain points:** lack of visibility into customer relationships; team skills and ramp ("reduce new hire ramp time, drive methodology adherence"); not knowing "why you win or lose deals"; deal health and churn ([product page](https://www.zoominfo.com/products/chorus)).
- **Capabilities:** capture and analyze calls, meetings, and emails; instant transcriptions and insights; automatic contact/communication sync "from the field to your CRM"; "Keep your finger on the pulse of every account and opportunity"; "measure deal momentum, mitigate churn, and action unbiased CRM data"; "Forecast with confidence"; win/loss discovery; call snippets shared with marketing/product/engineering ([product page](https://www.zoominfo.com/products/chorus)).
- **Shared memory evidence:** retained account/opportunity conversations are reused across coaching ("look back and see how I'm growing"), deal-momentum measurement, and win/loss trends — retained multi-deal conversation context reused across capabilities → **SM 1**. Analyst note: the page does not use the words "memory" or "knowledge" for this persistence.
- **Gaps:** no content library, roleplay, or AI agents documented for Chorus.
- **Source:** [zoominfo.com/products/chorus](https://www.zoominfo.com/products/chorus)

Change from batch 1: Traq.ai enters at 9; Momentum drops out of the top 10 (now 11). Rationale: Traq's verified call + deal + coaching overlap with a documented sales audience outweighs Momentum's thinner, CS-leaning evidence. Seismic + Highspot retains a top-10 slot as the strongest content-and-coaching benchmark among the researched companies — several others have positive content scores (Outreach, Attention, Allego, Mindtickle, Highspot's content products, and now Fireflies), but none combines that depth with enterprise enablement scale and roleplay. Jiminny (6 total) is the next challenger at 12, with Fireflies' upgraded sales bundle (6 total, adjacent) at 13.

### The 5 companies most focused on shared memory / connected sales context
1. **Sybill** — "every rep, agent, and workflow runs on shared memory" ([sybill.ai](https://www.sybill.ai/))
2. **Aida** — "the twin holds that memory permanently, carrying forward all the context" ([getaida.com/platform](https://getaida.com/platform))

### Market-gap updates (still hypotheses)
1. **Conversation memory + approved content (updated):** None of the nine batch-2 companies documents a searchable approved-content library, contextual content recommendations, or sales-material generation. The reviewed evidence does not yet establish how their cross-source context, organizational sharing, and governance compare with the memory layers documented for Gong, Sybill, Aida, and Backstory. Jiminny's calls/emails/CRM reuse and Noota's inbox/calendar/conversation briefs show multi-source retention emerging in the CI tier, but neither documents a governed, org-wide memory layer.
