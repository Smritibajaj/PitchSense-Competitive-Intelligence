## Batch 3 of 4: Sales Enablement & Content Intelligence — 6 companies (cumulative: 29 of 50)

**Research date:** October 18, 2026 (batches 1–2: October 17–18, 2026)

**Scope and framing:** Same standards as prior batches — official pages only, vendor claims not independently tested, 0 = "not verified in the reviewed sources" (not proven absence), the PitchSense narrative treated as vision, Shared Memory scored functionally, "documented audiences" vs "likely economic buyer (inference)" labels, announced-vs-GA distinctions, and claim-adjacent citations. Statements labeled "analyst note" are observations about page content, not vendor quotations.

**Entity resolution (major consolidation in this batch):**
1. **Showpad and Bigtincan have combined.** Vector Capital completed its acquisition of Showpad on October 30, 2025, having acquired Bigtincan in April 2025; the combined business "will operate under Showpad brand" ([completion release](https://www.showpad.com/press/showpad-and-bigtincan-merger-closed)). One entity.
2. **Brainshark is a Bigtincan-owned product** — current Bigtincan-branded material presents Brainshark as Bigtincan's readiness solution ("Bigtincan Readiness," [Brainshark update](https://www.bigtincan.com/resources/meet-the-brand-new-brainshark/)). Therefore Showpad + Bigtincan + Brainshark = **one company** (Showpad), researched as one entity with product-level subnotes. Analyst note: Bigtincan product capabilities are attributed to the merged entity without implying shared context across the acquired products.
3. **DocSend is a Dropbox product** — the fetched DocSend page is titled "DocSend is now part of Dropbox" ([Dropbox integration page](https://www.docsend.com/dropbox-integration/)). Researched as the DocSend offering of Dropbox; Dropbox-wide AI capabilities are not attributed to DocSend.
4. **Mediafly, SalesHood, Consensus, and Accent Technologies** have live official pages fetched on the research date; no acquisition or rebrand evidence surfaced in identity searches. Company-level deduplication beyond the documented combinations above remains provisional where ownership is unverified.

**Result:** the eight planned candidates collapse to six companies. Cumulative count: 23 + 6 = **29 vendor assessments**. The remaining batch-4 candidate list has been expanded to 21 names to preserve the 50-company target.

## Interim Synthesis
(Analyst assessment across the 29 researched vendor assessments — not full-landscape conclusions.)

### Cumulative top-10 relevance to PitchSense's product vision
1. **Sybill** — explicit shared-memory positioning ([sybill.ai](https://www.sybill.ai/))
2. **Aida** — digital twin with permanent cross-channel deal context ([getaida.com/platform](https://getaida.com/platform))
3. **Gong** — Revenue Graph persistent org context; enablement expansion ([Revenue Graph](https://www.gong.io/platform/revenue-graph))
4. **Showpad (incl. Bigtincan + Brainshark)** (new entry) — merged entity with strong content and coaching overlap plus partial call, deal, and foundation-context claims ([showpad.com](https://www.showpad.com/), [Genie](https://www.showpad.com/showpad-genie))
5. **Outreach** — agentic platform with approved-content grounding and KAIA CI ([outreach.ai](https://www.outreach.ai/))
6. **Allego** — broad verified span across conversation, content, coaching, and deal guidance ([allego.com](https://www.allego.com/))
7. **Salesloft + Clari** — unified execution + forecasting + CI ([merger page](https://www.salesloft.com/clari-salesloft-merger))
8. **Attention** — commitments-based memory, source-linked evidence, content generation ([attention.tech](https://www.attention.tech/))
9. **Mindtickle** — enablement + roleplay + content + CI + agentic layer ([mindtickle.com](https://www.mindtickle.com/))
10. **Traq.ai** — sales-focused CI with deal risk, forecasting, coaching ([traq.ai](https://www.traq.ai/))

Change from batch 2: Showpad enters at 4; Seismic + Highspot drops to 11. Rationale (analyst prioritization, not a verified scoring distinction — both companies currently receive SM 1): Showpad documents strong overlap in content intelligence and coaching (both 2) plus partial call capture, deal context, and a connected-foundation claim, at 2,000+ customer scale ([showpad.com](https://www.showpad.com/), [Genie](https://www.showpad.com/showpad-genie)) — it provides a useful content-and-coaching benchmark for the PitchSense vision. Seismic + Highspot covers the same two areas at similar strength ([completion release](https://www.seismic.com/newsroom/press-releases/seismic-completes-merger-with-highspot/)); the prioritization reflects Showpad's documented deal-context and conversation-capture claims, not a proven memory difference. Showpad's remaining gap is call intelligence (1) and unverified foundation reuse, which keeps it below Gong and the memory-first players.

### The 5 companies most focused on shared memory / connected sales context
1. **Sybill** — "every rep, agent, and workflow runs on shared memory" ([sybill.ai](https://www.sybill.ai/))
2. **Aida** — "the twin holds that memory permanently, carrying forward all the context" ([getaida.com/platform](https://getaida.com/platform))
3. **Gong** — Revenue Graph, "a centralized, secure network of your organization's revenue knowledge" ([gong.io](https://www.gong.io/platform/revenue-graph))
4. **Backstory (ex-People.ai)** — reads "every call, email, and CRM record across the quarter" ([backstory.ai](https://www.backstory.ai/))
5. **Attention** — retained, source-linked deal evidence reused for forecasting and agents ([attention.tech](https://www.attention.tech/)) — analyst judgment: the most memory-like of the SM-1 group

Showpad is the notable new challenger: its SM score is 1 (partial — a connected-context claim without documented accumulated reuse), so it stays outside the five; the distinction from the four SM-2 companies and Attention is that their retention-and-reuse mechanisms are documented at the feature level (Revenue Graph powering agents, twin memory, sourced answers, source-linked deal evidence).

### Most common competitive positioning themes (batch-3 additions)
1. **PE-driven enablement consolidation** — Vector Capital assembled Showpad + Bigtincan (incl.
