# Claim check

Source: the Appendix of `source/PitchSense_Product_Narrative_v1_text.md` ("What's built, in build and planned"), together with its "Main-flow claims that rest on in-build or planned items" list. The PDF in `source/` is authoritative.

1. **Extract claims.** List every statement that PitchSense does, has or delivers something. Include implied ones: a use-case row or a screenshot caption counts.
2. **Map each claim** to its Appendix item and give its status: **Built**, **In build**, **Planned**, or **Not in Narrative**.
3. **Rule by channel.**
   - Public copy may use Built claims only.
   - Proposals may use In build or Planned claims, each with a date.
   - A claim that is Not in Narrative is removed or sent to the product owner.
4. **Check the Narrative's flagged list.** If the copy uses any claim on the "rest on in-build or planned items" list, flag it by name.
5. **Check voice.** Look for the Narrative's "Words to avoid everywhere". In public copy, competitors are contrasted by category ("call recorders", "notetakers"), not by name.
6. **Check outcomes.** Every ROI figure, metric or customer result needs a named, real source. Without one, remove it.

Output a table (claim | Appendix item | status | allowed here? | fix), followed by the rewritten lines.

You are done when every claim in the copy appears in the table.
