### C3 — Pain-point patterns

- **Most batch 4–11 homepages state no explicit pain point.** The original capture found none on 20 of the 40. The Oct 19 gap-fill found one on Observe.AI's homepage and five more on each vendor's CRO page (Second Nature, Regie.ai, Wonderway, Mediafly, Reprise), so 14 of the 40 still state no pain on either page inspected. They lead with capability or category instead.
- Where a pain is stated, these themes recur:

  - **Buyer complexity and less selling time:** Humantic AI (buying committees), Seismic ("less time in front of buyers").

### C6 — Implications for the Phase 1 gap hypotheses

This is website-emphasis evidence only. It neither confirms nor refutes capability gaps.

1. **Conversation memory connected to approved content.**
   - Content-led vendors (PathFactory, Mediafly, Accent, Seismic, Showpad, Allego, Mindtickle) and conversation-led vendors still message separately.
   - Accent ("surface the right content, guide reps to the next best action") and Showpad (content + coaching signals + deal-stage context) come closest in copy.
   - The hypothesis stays open.
2. **A sales-org memory layer across calls, email, CRM and content.**
   - Memory wording is spreading to notetakers, scoped to meetings: Noota, Fathom, Otter.ai, Circleback, tl;dv.
   - It is also used at platform level in CX (Level AI) and in deal rooms (Aligned Brain™).
   - The *word* is commoditising faster than the *capability* is being shown. Phase 3 therefore treats memory as the mechanism behind the product, not the headline claim.
3. **Deal-grounded content generation with governance.**
   - No hero across the 50 claims both deal-grounded generation and governance over approved content.
   - Partial counterexamples remain Attention and Accent (Phase 1). PathFactory ("Stop building hundreds of one-off pages") addresses page generation on the marketing side.

### C7 — What the full-format upgrade adds (all 50, Oct 19, 2026)

Tables E and F now cover all 50 companies (homepage plus one CRO-relevant page each). Quotes below are verbatim from those tables; theme labels are analyst classification.

- **Source-linked answers are no longer rare.** Batches 1–3 had one source-cited answer demonstration (Backstory). Across the 50, these vendors now make an explicit source or evidence-link claim:
  - Noota: "Ask anything, get a sourced answer" and "Noota reads, answers and cites the calls it used."
  - Balto: answers that "links the calls behind each claim so you can check the work" (contact-centre insights page).
  - Siro: "Every score links to the moments behind it".
  - Humantic AI: "source provenance"; Quantified: "Drill from KPI to underlying evidence in two clicks"; Hyperbound: "every insight is tied back to actual activity".
  - Attention (product-UI demo with source and recording links) and Backstory (batch 2) as before.
- **Shared-memory wording now reaches the platform level.** Observe.AI's navigation names a platform layer "Shared memory and connective tissue". Sybill says "One shared understanding across your stack", Level AI "a single platform of shared intelligence" and Aligned "The Brain connects everything that touches your deal". Hyperbound's page connects "Calls, roleplays, email, CRM, and Slack" through "buyers, deals, and reps" (screen-reader-only text, not shown visually). Fireflies.ai says "Fireflies gives you perfect memory after every conversation."
- **Practice grounded in deal data is claimed.** PitchMonster: "Connect Salesforce and Gong to build role-plays in seconds from your live deal data". Salesforce's Agentforce FAQ describes Sales Coach role-play practice tailored to specific deals (Table E/F, batch 7).
- **CRO messaging centres on the forecast.** 24 of the 50 companies have a Table E claim that names the forecast. Pipeline, deal-risk, rep-performance and admin-burden themes follow; tool consolidation and cost appear for about a fifth of the set.
- **Content intelligence is the least claimed dimension:** 37 of 50 have Table F evidence for it, against 50 of 50 for cross-channel capture and 49 for less manual admin.

**Implications (testable, not verified absences).**
1. Phase 3 Direction A leans on source-backed answers. That angle now has at least six named counterexamples beyond Backstory, so it should be tested as a proof style, not claimed as a difference.
2. Gap hypothesis 2 (a sales-org memory layer) has new partial counterexamples (Observe.AI, Hyperbound, Sybill), in addition to the notetakers in C6. Copy remains the only evidence here; Phase 1 scores are unchanged.
3. Deal-grounded practice (PitchMonster, Salesforce Sales Coach) overlaps PitchSense's coaching story. Per Narrative v1, practice built from your own documents is Built, while a drill tied to a specific call is In build and real-call scoring is Planned.
