Where do my Meeting Reports get synced in HubSpot?

Read will sync Meeting Reports to every external attendee's Contact record as an 'Activity'. You'll also be able to find them on the Company record the Contact is associated with.

Can I selectively sync Meeting Reports to HubSpot?

Yes! You can have Read automatically sync your meetings to HubSpot, or you can manually select which meetings to sync using the sync button on the Meeting Report page
