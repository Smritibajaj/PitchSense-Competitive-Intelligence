FAQs

  • What is Humantic AI?

    -

    Humantic AI is a Buyer Intelligence platform that combines Account Intelligence (Agent Miia) and People Intelligence (Agent Pi) to help enterprise sellers research accounts, understand stakeholders, and personalize outreach, all at scale and in minutes rather than hours. Together, Miia + Pi deliver both the full business context of an account and the behavioral/personality signals of the people inside it.

  • What is Humantic AI’s primary value proposition?

    +

    Humantic AI provides highly actionable, enterprise-grade account research + best-in-class People Intelligence faster, cheaper and more consistently than manual research or single-pass LLM summaries offered by ChatGPT’s deep research. It leads to higher-quality pipeline, faster deal cycles, and more effective outreach across buying committees.

  • How do Agents Miia and Pi work together?

    +

    Miia gives the account context (why the company might buy); Pi gives the people context (how to sell to individual decision makers). Combined, you can map strategic priorities to specific stakeholder messaging and a tailored outreach cadence.

  • How is Humantic AI different from ChatGPT or generic LLMs?

    +

    ChatGPT leverages a powerful but general-purpose, one-size-fits-all LLM to provide its insights. Agents Miia and Pi by Humantic AI are purpose-built, multi-agent systems with specialized extractors, domain models, validation layers, and source provenance. This architecture yields enterprise-caliber, highly actionable and verifiable research tailored to sales context, not just generic business research.

  • Who benefits most from Humantic AI? (ICP)

    +

    Humantic AI is most valuable for enterprise sellers and revenue teams (AEs, SAMs, RevOps, Sales Enablement) who work on large, complex, multi-stakeholder deals. It also benefits sales development, customer success, and research teams at companies with high ticket-size deals and relationship driven selling. Companies with sales motions that are transactional in nature don’t see as big an impact from Humantic AI.

  • How do you know that Humantic AI delivers results?

    +

    Humantic AI is trusted by top sales teams worldwide. Many of them have objectively measured Humantic AI’s impact on pipeline and win rates and shared them publicly. You can see more of them in these videos , or check out the Humantic AI Results Book in the Resource Library . Humantic AI is also one of the very few Sales AI tools that’s been recognized by Gartner as a Cool Vendor. It has regularly been a top 5 Sales AI on G2, including having the honor of being a Top 100 Highest Satisfaction product in the world for 2025 (out of 125,000 products). Customers switching from competitors like Crystal Knows report higher accuracy, better adoption, and improved deal conversion.
