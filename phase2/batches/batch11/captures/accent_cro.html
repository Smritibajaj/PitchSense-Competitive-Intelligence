Accent Manage

Get a clear picture of how you are really selling.

Seller and team performance

See not just how active your reps are, but how effective, because activity alone doesn't show who's actually moving deals forward. Scorecards and clear visuals make the difference obvious.

Account and opportunity health

Accent Manage highlights

What sales management actually gives you.

Accent sales management shows you exactly what is happening across your selling motion and turns it into clear next steps. Accent AI sifts the data, surfaces the insights, and recommends the next move, while you stay in control of the call.

See team performance

Know exactly how your team is performing at the individual, team, and organization levels. Follow the thread from activity through to effectiveness and results, and see why your top reps win while others stall.

Get the insights to improve

Because Accent AI analyzes the full picture, it surfaces clear areas to improve at every level, rep, team, and organization, including pipeline and forecasting. You get the where and the why, not just another dashboard.

Automate your coaching

Accent Assist AI works like a coach in the flow of the deal, giving reps recommendations based on your own sales process, instead of generic best practice. It can even take on different coaching styles to motivate and push the team.

Improve your pipeline health

Learn to tell a healthy deal from an unhealthy one at a glance. Pipeline health improves fast as your team spends its time on the deals that can actually close, instead of feeding the ones that never will.

Increase your forecast accuracy

The bottom line is a forecast you can defend. See the number clearly enough to make the adjustments that get you there, and cut the noise so the team stays focused on what matters.

See it before it slips

Visibility that makes deal health obvious at a glance.

Because clear dashboards track every account and deal, you always see who is engaged, who has gone quiet, and the real health of each opportunity at a glance.

Deal health, updated daily

Never manage off a stale number.

Every account and opportunity gets re-scored daily, not just when someone remembers to update it. Because your view always reflects this week’s activity instead of last quarter’s, you catch a stalling deal while there’s still time to save it.

Ready anywhere they work.

Deep integrations and plug-ins put Accent inside the tools your team already uses, anywhere, anytime.

Stand behind the number

Walk into the forecast call ready for pushback.

A number you can’t explain falls apart the moment a CFO or the board asks a real question about it. Because every forecast is backed by the actual facts on which deals are real, you can defend the number in the room, not just report it and hope nobody digs deeper.
