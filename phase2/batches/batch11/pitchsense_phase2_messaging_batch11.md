# PitchSense AI — Phase 2: Competitor Website Messaging and Landing Page Analysis (Batch 11)

> **Scope change, Oct 20, 2026:** Reprise, Consensus, Dropbox (DocSend) were removed from the 50-company scope (swapped for Granola, Read AI, Day.ai, Oliv.ai, Glean and Spekit, which fit the Product Narrative better). This report is kept as the dated Oct 19 record and still covers them; the batch CSVs no longer include them.

**Research date:** October 19, 2026 (Europe/Berlin). Homepages were captured as HTML for the light-format pass; the batch was upgraded to the full format the same day, adding one CRO-relevant supporting page per company.

**Scope:** Yoodli (https://yoodli.ai/; CRO page: https://yoodli.ai/for-business/revenue-teams); Accent Technologies (https://accent-technologies.com/; CRO page: https://accent-technologies.com/sales-management/); Consensus (https://goconsensus.com/; CRO page: https://goconsensus.com/solutions/sales); Reprise (https://www.reprise.com/; CRO page: https://www.reprise.com/solutions/sales-leaders); Dropbox (DocSend) (https://www.dropbox.com/docsend; CRO page: https://www.dropbox.com/business/solutions/sales). Phase 1 discovery, classification and capability scoring are not repeated here.

**Method and verification scope.** Heading levels were verified against HTML tags in the saved pages, in document order; nav, mega-menu, footer, tab and responsive duplicates were excluded. Every exact-copy field (Table A hero fields, Table B headings and subheadings, Table D CTA labels, Table E claims and the quoted text in Table F) was string-matched against the saved captures after normalising whitespace, HTML entities and curly quotes; values split across inline tags were matched word by word and are noted in their rows. CTA destinations are the `href` in the HTML; destinations that were not fetched are marked "intent unverified". All captures are in `captures/` in this folder.

**Limitations.** (1) HTML only: no rendered-browser check was possible for this batch, so visual hero media, client-side rotating headlines and script-rendered content are labelled as such rather than confirmed. (2) Table A hero fields equal the light-format row (`website_messaging_batch11.csv`); Not-verified light fields were re-checked on Oct 19, 2026 and any field filled is noted in that row's Verification cell as "Gap-fill". (3) Table F was written directly against PitchSense Product Narrative v1 (Sep 30, 2026); its PitchSense column is copied verbatim from batches 1–3. (4) Vendor metrics, testimonials and badges are vendor claims, recorded verbatim and not verified. Messaging emphasis is not evidence that a capability is missing.

**Canonical data:** the `table_*.csv` files in this folder. This report renders them; if a table changes, re-render the table sections.

## Final Strategic Analysis

All statements below are analyst interpretation grounded in the tables above. Vendor claims are the vendors' own statements, preserved verbatim; they are not independently verified. CTA frequency is recorded, not treated as evidence of conversion effectiveness. No PitchSense landing-page copy is written here.

### 1. Dominant homepage narrative of each competitor

- **Yoodli** runs a human-skills narrative: "The AI Platform for Human Skills" above the H1 "AI that helps your team", with "One end-to-end platform for organization-wide enablement, powered by AI roleplays." ([homepage](https://yoodli.ai/)). Sales is one of six use cases, next to onboarding, managers, tool rollouts, healthcare and partners.
- **Accent Technologies** runs a consolidation narrative: "Every tool your team needs to sell. On one platform." and "Built to replace ten tools, not add an eleventh." ([homepage](https://accent-technologies.com/)), with content, AI answers, guided selling and readiness as the four modules.
- **Consensus** runs a demo-to-revenue narrative: "Unleash your demo, unlock revenue." across "agentic, self-guided, and live" demos, closing on "Turn every demo into pipeline" ([homepage](https://goconsensus.com/)).

- **Dropbox (DocSend)** runs a control-and-insight narrative: "Securely share files with real-time control and insights" ([Dropbox product page](https://www.dropbox.com/docsend)), framed as an upgrade from email attachments.

### 2. How each communicates business value before features

- **Yoodli** puts customer outcomes ahead of the product: the metric section "Yoodli drives real impact across organizations and industries" and the testimonial carousel come before the module tour ("One system, powered by AI roleplays, that drives measurable outcomes") ([homepage](https://yoodli.ai/)).
- **Accent** defines the category first ("What is revenue enablement?"), states the problem ("Accent believes teams should work off the same information, not three versions of the truth."), then names the gap it closes ("Closing the data-to-deals gap.") before listing modules ([homepage](https://accent-technologies.com/)).
- **Consensus** leads with revenue in the H1 and shows deal-value proof ("Customers see the impact") before its Demo Intelligence explanation; most of its platform detail is rendered client-side and could not be verified ([homepage](https://goconsensus.com/)).
- **Reprise** goes to capability almost at once (data injection, tours, clones, video) and states its business outcome only in the closing block: "Accelerate deals and increase win rates with the leading enterprise AI demo platform." ([homepage](https://www.reprise.com/)).
- **DocSend** states value in the hero paragraph ("Enjoy complete control over your most sensitive files after hitting send and sharpen your business proposals with valuable insights.") and moves straight to feature tabs ([Dropbox product page](https://www.dropbox.com/docsend)).

### 3. Who communicates shared memory most effectively

Analyst judgment about communication, not measured effectiveness: **Accent** is closest. It frames one shared information base for every go-to-market team ("not three versions of the truth") and an AI that "learns your products, services, accounts, and pipeline" ([homepage](https://accent-technologies.com/)). **Consensus** is second with a single-stream framing, "Every interaction, one stream of intelligence.", but the stream is limited to demo interactions ([homepage](https://goconsensus.com/)). **Reprise** frames a shared asset library ("A Single Library for Your Entire Go-To-Market") and points its MCP at "call transcripts and internal notes" as personalisation input, which is data access rather than memory ([homepage](https://www.reprise.com/)). Yoodli and DocSend frame no shared layer on the inspected pages. None of the five uses memory language.
