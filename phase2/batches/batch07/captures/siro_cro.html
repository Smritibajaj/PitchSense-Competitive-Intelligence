Scorecards

Scorecards built around how your team sells.

Generic sales advice doesn't help a team with its own playbook. You decide what a great conversation looks like, and Siro scores every conversation against it.

The right scorecard, automatically

Build scorecards around your own process and change them anytime. Siro recognizes what kind of conversation it is, whether a first visit, a follow-up or a phone call, and scores it against the right scorecard on its own.

See why every score is what it is

Click any score to see the moments behind it. Managers and sellers understand exactly why a conversation scored the way it did, and what to do differently next time.

A coaching guide, not a grade

Scores point managers to where a seller needs help and show how they're improving week over week. If you'd rather sellers not see their scores, your customer success manager can turn them off.

Reports

Reports that do the digging for you.

Most reporting waits for you to go looking. Siro sends the answers to you: who needs attention, on which skill, and where to start, on whatever cadence fits how you run your team.

From hours of recordings to answers in seconds

See which sellers need attention and on which skill, then jump straight to the moments that show it. No more spending a weekend replaying appointments.

Delivered on your cadence

Get scheduled reports every morning, on Mondays, or every month. Have a question about what you're seeing? Ask Siro right from the report.

Progress over time

Compare each seller's scores week over week, and use leaderboards to see who's building better habits across teams and locations.
