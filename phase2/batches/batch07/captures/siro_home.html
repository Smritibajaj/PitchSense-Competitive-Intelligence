Most sales teams today still run on guesswork.

The conversations that decide a sale contain some of the most valuable data a business has. Today, most of it isn't captured. What's left for sales teams often relies on guessing.

Performance samples

Ride-alongs, mystery shops and spot-checked calls catch a handful of conversations and get treated as the whole picture.

Generic playbooks

Training built on someone else's methodology and tribal knowledge, not on what your best sellers actually say.

Limited CRM notes

A long conversation becomes three lines typed from memory, with the detail that mattered gone.

It’s fast and iterative.

The more a seller records, the faster they improve. Siro shows up before, during and right after every conversation, to help close the deal and coach on what just happened.

It’s personal.

Sales science without Siro is generic, built on someone else’s deals, process and industry. Siro’s science comes from each seller’s own conversations.

It’s scalable.

AI meets every seller in the moment, so improvement isn’t capped by a coach’s ceiling or anyone’s physical bandwidth.

What customers love about Siro

Questions about AI sales coaching with Siro

Siro is an end-to-end sales performance system. Sellers record conversations on their device, and Siro uses what was said to handle notes, follow-ups and CRM updates, score each conversation against your process, and coach every seller.

Most AI sales coaching tools were built for video calls and inside sales teams. Siro handles those too, but was optimized for sellers on-the-go. They can record from our app on their phone or tablet, even without signal, and Siro’s AI supports them before, during, and after every conversation.

There are other products built for in-person conversations. Siro is the platform built for adoption and focused on results. See How It Works

Yes, Siro is built for adoption. Many customers have initial concerns that their team will think of recording as “big brother,” but today tens of thousands of conversations are recorded daily.

To roll out Siro, our customer success team helps you stick the landing during onboarding. Whether you have a team of 5 or 5,000, we know how to run a successful implementation, across industries and across different company cultures.

Yes, it’s legal to record sales conversations. Some countries and states have “two party consent” laws, meaning a customer needs to agree to being recorded, Siro works there too.

Siro has been used to record millions of conversations in both one and two-party consent regions. For more, read our state-by-state guide.
