Sales solution in Microsoft Copilot

Designed for sellers, sales solution in Microsoft Copilot brings sales data and insights in the flow of work to help sellers save time, focus on customers, and close more deals. Sales solution in Microsoft Copilot is experienced within Microsoft 365 and connects directly to Dynamics and Salesforce CRMs for real-time data and updates.
