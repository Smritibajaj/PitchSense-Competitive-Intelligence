What is Noota? An AI note taker and meeting assistant for your whole workday

Noota is an AI collaborator that records, transcribes and summarizes your meetings, calls and interviews, then does the follow-up work for you: structured meeting notes, action items assigned to the right people, follow-up emails and updates to your CRM or ATS.
‍

Unlike a simple AI note taker, Noota works before, during and after each meeting. It prepares a brief from your past calls and emails, captures the conversation on Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, the phone or in person, and keeps everything searchable in one workspace. More than 200,000 users rely on it today.
‍

See the AI Notetaker

How does an AI note taker work, and why use one?

An AI note taker listens to a conversation, turns speech into a written transcript, identifies who said what, and uses AI to write a summary, key decisions and next steps. You stop typing during the call and stay focused on the people in front of you.
‍

With Noota, the meeting report follows the template you choose (sales discovery, recruiting interview, customer success review, one-to-one…), timestamps link every point back to the recording, and the follow-up email is drafted before you leave the call. Teams typically use it to save admin time and keep a reliable record of every conversation.

Does Noota work with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Connect your Google or Outlook calendar and Noota records your Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams meetings automatically, or only the ones you choose. You can also paste a meeting link to send Noota to a call that is not in your calendar.
‍

Noota also records phone calls from a dedicated business number and in-person conversations with the mobile app, so every conversation ends up in the same place with the same transcript and report.

Can Noota record meetings without a bot joining the call?

Yes. With the Noota Desktop app, meetings are recorded directly from your computer: no bot appears in the participant list, and you still get the full transcript, meeting summary and action items.
‍

If you prefer a visible assistant, Noota can also join as a bot. You choose, meeting by meeting, from your calendar or from the notification Noota Desktop shows a minute before each call.
‍

Can I transcribe an audio or video file I already have?

Yes. Upload an mp3, mp4, m4a, wav or webm recording from your Noota workspace: Noota transcribes it and writes the report in a few minutes.

Can I record and transcribe in-person meetings on my phone?

Yes. The Noota mobile app for iPhone and Android records interviews, site visits and face-to-face meetings, then produces the same transcript, summary and tasks as for your video calls. Everything syncs to your workspace, so your in-person conversations are searchable next to your online meetings.
‍

Which languages does Noota transcribe?

Noota transcribes meetings in many languages and detects the spoken language automatically, including French, English and Spanish. The meeting summary is written in the language of the conversation, and a report can be translated in one click when your team works across countries.

What does a Noota meeting report include?

Each report contains an AI summary, the decisions made, next steps with owners and due dates, and chapters of the conversation, with timestamps that jump to the exact moment in the recording. Depending on your template, it can also include a scorecard, speaker talk time and a ready-to-send follow-up email.
‍

Reports use your own templates: you can pick one from the library, describe a new one in plain language, or build it from an existing document.

Can I search my past meetings and ask questions about them?

Yes. Ask Noota answers questions across all your meetings and emails, with the sources it used: “Prep my next meeting with Acme”, “What did we promise this supplier this month?”, “Draft a follow-up for today's sync”. Ready-made agents cover sales, recruiting, customer success and management, and scheduled actions deliver a digest automatically every week.
‍

Which tools does Noota integrate with?

Noota connects to your calendar (Google and Outlook), your email (Gmail and Outlook), video conferencing (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams), more than 80 ATS and several CRMs, so notes and tasks land where your team already works. Reports can also be sent to Slack, Notion or Google Drive.
‍

Developers can use the Noota API and webhooks, and the Noota MCP server connects your meetings to AI assistants such as Claude.

Is Noota GDPR compliant, and where is my data hosted?

Yes. Noota is a European company and your data is hosted in Europe. Noota is GDPR and AI Act compliant, follows ISO 27001-aligned practices, encrypts data at rest and in transit, and offers a SecNumCloud-compatible architecture for organizations that need sovereign deployment.
‍

Permissions let you decide who sees each meeting, and you can keep reports private or share them with a link, a Slack channel or your CRM.
