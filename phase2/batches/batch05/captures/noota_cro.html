What does the Sales Agent do, exactly?

It covers the sales workflow from the first touch to the decision. It runs prospecting sequences, records and analyses discovery calls and meetings, coaches the rep during the call, scores each deal from what was said, and writes everything into your CRM. Your reps sell, Noota does the rest.

How does live coaching work?

You configure the key phrases that matter for your sales motion: a competitor's name, a pricing question, a compliance requirement. When a prospect says one of them, Noota guides the rep on the next step, in real time, without leaving the call. After the call, the analysis shows the objections, the buying signals and the talk ratio.

How is the deal score calculated?

From the conversations: the needs expressed, the objections raised and answered, the buying signals, the next steps agreed. The score moves after each call and comes with its reasons, so a manager can see why a deal went up or down and what to do next.

Which CRMs does it update?

HubSpot and Salesforce first, and more than 5,000 tools through the integrations. The summary, the stage, the next steps and the score are written into the deal record after each conversation. Nothing to type.

What does a sales manager get out of it?

Every call analysed, not just the few a manager has time to listen to. Winning patterns and weak points across the team, objections that come back, and deal scores that can be explained. Coaching becomes a matter of data, not of memory.
