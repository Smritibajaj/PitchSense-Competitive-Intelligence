FAQs

What is Balto and how does it work with my contact center?

Balto is an AI platform that integrates directly with your existing phone systems. It listens to conversations in real time, provides real-time guidance to agents, automates coaching and QA, and deploys fully automated AI voice agents, all from a single platform.

How does Balto's AI voice agent differ from a traditional IVR?

Unlike traditional IVR systems that rely on rigid menus and scripts, Balto's AI voice agent, Togo, uses large language models trained on your best agent conversations. It handles natural, dynamic interactions, adapts to customer intent, and achieves up to 70% call containment, all without sounding robotic.

Which contact center platforms does Balto integrate with?

Balto connects natively with NICE, Genesys, RingCentral, 8x8, Convoso, and many more. We have prebuilt integrations with over 60 providers and are constantly building new and expansive integrations that fit into any environment. We also allow for call ingestion for those on old systems with limited APIs.

Is Balto compliant with HIPAA, PCI DSS, and SOC 2?

Yes. Balto is SOC 2 Type II certified, HIPAA compliant, and PCI DSS certified, making it the trusted choice for healthcare, financial services, and other regulated industries where data security is paramount.

How quickly can we get Balto up and running?

Most customers are live within 30 days. Larger rollouts often run longer by design, and we'll set that pace with you. Your implementation team handles the technical integration and configures playbooks, coaching templates, and QA scorecards alongside your team before launch.
