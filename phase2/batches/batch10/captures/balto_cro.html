FAQs

How does Kodi know which data to use?

Kodi is grounded in your own Balto data, scorecards, playbooks, call transcripts, and agent history. It picks the scorecard the question implies, shows every step it took, and links the calls behind each claim so you can check the work.

Do I have to write triggers for an experiment?

No. Balto detects the calls where the objection or behavior comes up on its own. You review the language, choose how it gets delivered, and launch.

What is the difference between real-time guidance and post-call coaching?

Real-time guidance appears to the agent during the live call, the moment the situation is detected. Post-call coaching arrives after relevant calls as a reviewable task tied to a specific moment in the recording.

How is the result measured?

Each experiment starts a 30-day measurement in the opportunity it targets, compared against your recent baseline rather than a team average.
