FAQs

How does Salesloft help improve sales forecast accuracy?

Salesloft's AI is trained on your historical deal data and layered with live rep and activity signals, so your forecast is grounded in fact, not gut feel.

How does Salesloft use AI for sales forecasting?

Salesloft's AI analyzes historical deal data alongside real-time pipeline activity to surface an accurate call number, flag at-risk deals, and recommend next best actions — automating sales forecasting in the parts that used to rely on manual judgment.

Why is Salesloft the best sales forecasting software?

Salesloft is the only platform that combines AI-powered revenue forecasting, real-time deal tracking, and integrated execution in one place. You don't just get accurate revenue projections — you get the workflows to act on them, without ever leaving the platform.

Can reps and managers use Salesloft Forecast without RevOps support?

Yes. Salesloft lets anyone ask plain-language questions about revenue data and get instant answers — no RevOps ticket or stale report required.
