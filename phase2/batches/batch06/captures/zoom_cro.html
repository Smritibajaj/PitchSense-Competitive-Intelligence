Revenue leaders

Get real-time, data-backed insights that drive growth

See your entire pipeline clearly with Zoom Revenue Accelerator. Make more informed strategic decisions with AI-enhanced deal intelligence and a forecast grounded in what's actually happening inside your deals.

Contact sales Contact sales

Know exactly where your deals stand

To keep opportunities moving forward, you need to know where to focus your attention. Revenue Accelerator uses AI to analyze sales conversations and deal activity to see which deals are moving forward and which have stalled, so you can forecast with confidence and put your energy where it counts.

See the big picture across your teams

Revenue Accelerator pulls pipeline trends, team performance, and forecast commitments into one unified view — so you see what's working across regions and teams, and where to steer your quarterly plan.

Get deal updates in seconds

No need to chase down reps or sit through long meetings just to understand a deal's status. Get quick, automated summaries of deal health and rep activity that you can read in moments, giving you the context you need to make faster, more informed decisions.
