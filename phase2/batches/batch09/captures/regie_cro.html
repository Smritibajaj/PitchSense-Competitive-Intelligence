Why RegieGO

Drowning in tasks. Starving for conversations.

RegieGO is an agentic sales workspace built for sellers. Half your day goes to tabs and list prep before you say hello to anyone worth talking to. RegieGO's agents run the research, list building, dialing, and follow-up on a schedule, so you start the day with a queue instead of a to-do list.

You get more at-bats than your headcount can explain, and every one is researched before you pick up the phone.

Messaging

Written in your voice, sent from your address

Your buyers are buried in AI slop, and anything that reads like it was canned goes straight into that pile. RegieGO learns your voice from your own sent mail and sends from your connected Gmail or Outlook, not a vendor burner domain, so your message arrives from your address, in your writing, so your sending reputation stays yours.

  • Written from your own sent mail, so it reads like you wrote it
  • Arrives from your address, so replies land where you read them
  • Every message names the why-now behind it: a funding round, a new exec, company news

Every channel

Phone, email, and LinkedIn in one queue

Touch after touch goes out and buyers still answer like they have never heard of you. Most sequencers answer that with a cadence you build and then maintain. RegieGO re-reads what has actually happened on each account and builds that day's list around whoever is worth the effort now, across all three channels: emails send from your own inbox, calls land in the dialer, and LinkedIn touches come to you as tasks. Nothing goes out under your name that you did not send.

  • Calls land in the dialer with local presence, DNC, and dispositions handled
  • LinkedIn touches come to you as tasks you send yourself, never bot messages
  • Nothing goes out under your name that you did not send

Setup

Nothing to wire up before you can prospect

RegieGO reads your website, builds a profile of what you sell and who it is for, and proposes the titles and company sizes to search before you have configured anything. Connect your inbox and day one is outreach, not setup.

  • A built-in CRM from day one, so you can start with nothing
  • Local dialer numbers you pick by area code, no carrier setup
