From Vanity Metrics to Real Insights

Without PathFactory

You're stuck with shallow metrics like clicks, downloads, and MQLs. You see that a lead engaged, but you have no idea with what, for how long, or what it means for your pipeline.

With PathFactory

You see the entire journey. Our platform measures the time spent on every asset, tracks the bingeable path, and rolls up engagement to the account level, revealing true buying intent.

Features

The Revenue Intelligence Engine

Account-Level Roll-up

Move beyond individual lead scores. We automatically connect every visitor to their account and roll up engagement data to show you the activity of the entire buying committee.

Real-Time Journey Tracking

See the complete, chronological path a buyer takes through your content. Understand which assets are accelerating deals and where buyers are dropping off.

Asset & Topic Analytics

Get detailed performance data on every piece of content. Understand which topics and formats are resonating most with your highest-value accounts.

CRM & MAP Integration

Sync every engagement signal and account-level insight directly to your CRM and MAP, delivering actionable data where your revenue teams already work.
