FAQs

The Questions
We’re Asked Most

Answers to what life sciences and commercial leaders ask most about AI sales coaching.

What is AI sales coaching?

AI sales coaching is the use of artificial intelligence to personalize practice, preparation, feedback, and reinforcement for every sales rep. Unlike one-time training, it is continuous and adapts to each rep’s role, experience, proficiency, and business context, helping reps build the skills they need for the conversations that matter most.

How does AI sales coaching work?

It combines realistic AI-powered roleplay, personalized coaching paths, and real-time feedback into a continuous workflow. Quantified brings practice, certification, field coaching, compliance, and reporting together on one platform, so every rep is coached against the same approved content and the same standards, from onboarding through every launch.

How is AI roleplay different from traditional sales roleplay?

Traditional roleplay is hard to scale, inconsistent across managers, and often limited to workshops, ride-alongs, or certification events. Quantified gives every rep realistic, repeatable practice against the conversations they actually face, with feedback tied to your approved messaging, coaching standards, and readiness goals.

What makes Quantified different from general-purpose AI tools?

Quantified is purpose-built for life sciences and regulated commercial environments, not adapted from horizontal AI. It runs on a fine-tuned private model, paired with an MLR-aware governance layer that keeps messaging on-label and audit-ready.

Does Quantified integrate with Salesforce, Veeva, and other systems?

Yes. Quantified integrates with the systems life sciences and commercial teams already use, including Salesforce, Veeva, LMS platforms, and approved-content management systems. See full integration details.

How fast can teams see results with AI sales coaching?

Quantified customers have seen up to a 40% reduction in time to readiness, up to 6× more rep practice, and up to a 19% lift in good selling outcomes. Results can come fast: Self-Authoring turns approved content into practice scenarios in days, and Sanofi certified 100% of its 500-rep field force in five days with four trainers.
