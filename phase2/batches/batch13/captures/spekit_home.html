Common Questions About Spekit

Spekit is an AI-native revenue enablement platform built for in-workflow execution. It delivers governed GTM knowledge, agentic coaching, deal context, and personalized buyer experiences inside the tools reps already use, at the moment they need them. The platform is built on four pillars: the GTM Knowledge Engine, Agentic Coaching & Actions, Personalized Buyer Experiences, and Unified Deal Context.
